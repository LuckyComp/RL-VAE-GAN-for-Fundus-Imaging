## Encoder Pre-training
As the GAN system relies on the latent space generated by the Encoder, we must either first train the encoder and then proceed to use the weights again during generation of images, or both encoder and SRGAN are trained sequential. In this project we choose to employ the first method rather than the second as it reduces the chance of failures during training, and reduces training times.
The VAE architecture is set up, with 4 Conv2d layers that compress the dimension of the image, followed by a Average Adaptive Pool that further standardizes the latent space dimension so as to minimize issues caused by variation in input dimensions. The decoder follows a similar structure with a upscale layer followed by 4 ConvTranspose2d layers. In the scope of this project the decoder is auxilary to us as we do not wish to reconstruct the image using the decoder.
### Loss Calculation
As is common in VAE we use ELBO loss (Evidence Lower Bound) which is a combination of two losses, a reconstruction loss that affects the decoder, and a KL-Divergence loss that affects the encoder.
$$L_{ELBO} = L_{recon} + \beta \cdot L_{KL}$$
Reconstruction loss is calculated by combining MSE for pixel-to-pixel comparisions and SSIM for semantic errors during reconstruction, $L_{recon} = 1 \cdot MSE_{mean} + (0.5) \cdot (1 - SSIM)$.
